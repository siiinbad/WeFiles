Privacy & data

WeFiles application privacy information · Updated October 27, 2026

What this workspace stores

Your email, username, optional display name, chosen avatar icon, password hash, account status, uploaded files, messages, saved files, notifications, and timestamps. Authentication protection also records limited login-attempt information, including IP addresses and sign-in identifiers.

Why we use it

To authenticate you, review membership, share files and conversations with approved members, deliver essential account emails, and protect the workspace. Other members see your username/display name and shared content. Workspace administrators see account emails and membership details.

Storage and service providers

Files are stored privately and downloads require approval. The hosting operator controls the application database, private file storage, email delivery provider, and backups. Optional error monitoring is enabled only when the operator configures it. This application does not sell personal data or include advertising or marketing analytics.

Cookies and preferences

Essential session and CSRF cookies support login and secure forms. Your theme choice is saved in your browser’s local storage. There are no optional tracking cookies in the default application.

Retention and deletion

Your account data remains while your account exists. Account deletion removes your uploaded files, messages, saved files, and notifications, and anonymizes your audit attribution. Shared folders remain. File deletions are queued and retried if storage is temporarily unavailable. Security and audit records are pruned according to the operator’s configured retention schedule. Backups expire according to the hosting operator’s backup policy and must not be restored into service without reapplying erasures.

Your choices

You can edit your username/display name, change your password, and delete your account in Account settings. Contact your operator to request access, corrections, an export, or help with an email change.
